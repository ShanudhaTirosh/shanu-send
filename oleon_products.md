## Executive Overview

Oleon Workspace (by Olee AI) is a unified Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and messaging platform designed for businesses whose customer interactions happen through modern messaging apps. It consolidates conversations, contacts, orders, and automated AI agents into a single record across channels like **WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, and Web Chat**.

**Core Motto:** *"Turn on the parts you need. It started as one inbox. It is now everything a business does between the first message and the repeat order, and every part of it works on the conversation your customer already started."*

### <a id="chat"></a>Chat (`chat`)

- **Category:** Answer them
- **URL:** [https://oleon.io/products/chat](https://oleon.io/products/chat)
- **Tagline:** *One inbox, and every channel arrives in it.*
- **Meta Description:** A shared inbox where WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram and web chat land together, with owners, labels, saved replies and the whole history searchable.

#### Overview
Most teams answer customers in four apps on one person's phone. Chat puts every channel in one place, gives each conversation an owner, and keeps the history where the next person can find it.

#### Key Capabilities & Detailed Features
- **A conversation has an owner.**  
  Assign it, and everybody can see who has it. That is the difference between a shared inbox and four people typing the same reply, and it is the first thing a team of more than two needs.
- **Labels carry an owner too.**  
  A label is a saved thing with a name, a colour and an icon, not free text somebody retypes slightly differently each week. Apply it from the conversation, filter the list by it, and the list stays worth filtering.
- **Search reaches the whole history.**  
  Not the last few hundred messages the browser happened to keep. The search asks the server, so a conversation from a year ago answers the same as one from this morning.
- **It reads Sinhala, Tamil and English.**  
  Including Singlish typed on an English keyboard and sent in Sinhala script. The three scripts need real shaping to render at all, which is why most tools show boxes and this one does not.
- **Delivered, read, and who is there now.**  
  Ticks on the message and presence on the person, both live. An agent can see the customer is reading rather than guess at it.

#### Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Can two agents answer the same customer?**  
They can, and the conversation records who sent what. Assigning it is how you stop them needing to, and the assignment is visible in the list rather than agreed in another chat.

**Q: Does it work on a phone?**  
Yes. The workspace installs to the home screen and runs full screen with a tab bar, and the chat takes the whole display. It cannot notify a phone whose screen is off yet, so it is a working app rather than a pager.

**Q: What happens to the history if we stop paying?**  
Conversations are records. They are kept, and they are exportable. Nothing in the product deletes a message a customer sent you.

- **Category:** Answer them
- **URL:** [https://oleon.io/products/connect](https://oleon.io/products/connect)
- **Tagline:** *Plug in the channels you already answer on.*
- **Meta Description:** Connect WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram and web chat to one inbox, with a guided setup for each and a live status that tells you before one breaks.

#### Overview
Every channel has its own setup, its own approval and its own way of going quiet. Connect is a wizard for each one, and a status that says which is working right now.

#### Key Capabilities & Detailed Features
- **A wizard for each channel, not a settings page.**  
  Each connection has steps, and each step tells you what it is asking for. Connecting a business number is a sequence of approvals by somebody else, so the screen shows where you are in it rather than a form that fails at the end.
- **Told before it breaks.**  
  A channel's standing is read from the provider, not guessed from the last failed send. When a number is restricted or a token is expiring, it says so while there is still time to act.
- **Every channel belongs to a project.**  
  One business, one set of channels, one inbox. Which matters the moment you run two brands, because the alternative is one inbox holding both and an agent replying to the wrong customer as the wrong company.
- **Turn the machines off without disconnecting.**  
  One switch silences every automatic reply on a channel and leaves the channel connected. For the afternoon a real person needs to handle everything, which is not the same as taking the number down.

- **Meta Description:** An AI agent grounded in your own material, in Sinhala, Tamil and English, that looks things up mid-conversation and hands over to a person when it should.

#### Overview
An agent is only worth having if it is right, and right means answering from your material rather than inventing something plausible. Scout answers from what you gave it, and when it cannot, it fetches a person.

#### Key Capabilities & Detailed Features

  Answers come from the material you loaded. An agent that fills a gap with a confident guess costs more than the questions it answered, because the guess arrives looking exactly like the truth.
- **It hands over to a person.**  
  Handover is a built-in step rather than a fallback nobody configured. The conversation moves to the inbox with everything already said in it, so the agent taking over does not start by asking the customer to repeat themselves.
- **It looks things up mid-chat.**  
  Order status, a balance, whatever your system knows. The lookup happens inside the conversation, and what it returned is written down in the records afterwards.
- **Three languages, properly.**  
  Sinhala, Tamil and English, including a customer who types Singlish and expects Sinhala back. This is the part most agents skip, and the part most Sri Lankan customers notice first.
- **Every change is a new version.**  
  A live agent is never edited in place. A change is a copy, and publishing it is a separate, deliberate act, so nobody breaks the bot that is talking to customers right now.

- **Tagline:** *SMS, on the account you already pay for.*
- **Meta Description:** Send SMS through your own gateway account rather than ours: your sender name, your rates, your relationship with the provider, inside the same inbox.

#### Overview
Most businesses here already have an SMS account with a local provider, a sender name that took weeks to approve, and a rate they negotiated. There is no reason to buy all of that again from us. Connect the account you have.

#### Key Capabilities & Detailed Features
- **Your account, your rate.**  
  We do not resell messages. You keep the provider relationship, the approved sender name and the price you agreed, and Oleon is the thing that sends through it.
